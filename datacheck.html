Data Check — the audit script

A damage estimate is only as credible as its inputs. 07_datacheck.js is a read-only inspector that lays out every unit cost, damage curve and threshold — with its source — and regression-tests the valuation engine. The full source is embedded below.

What the script checks

SectionContent
Unit CostsThe 100 %-damage reconstruction cost of every class, tagged with provenance: [G] statistics-based, [E] recovery-budget request, [Furukawa] expert proposal — traceable to dataset_inventory.xlsx and the government source PDFs.
DDF CurvesAll 13 depth–damage functions (JRC Huizinga 2017, Asia set) plotted from their control points.
Province RatioThe BPS IKK 2025 construction-cost index per province (DKI Jakarta = 1.00).
ExposureThe 37 exposure assets with feature counts, so missing or stale layers are caught before an event.
Sanity TestA worked example — one house in Jakarta flooded to 1.5 m — computed live, verifiable by hand.
Engine TestPixel-wise vs. scalar DDF agreement, a hand-calculated damage case, the WorldPop scale test (guards against a ×100 population bug), and old-vs-new engine totals.

The app below is the script running live — it reads constants and assets only, performs no batch computation and writes nothing.
