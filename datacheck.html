Data Check — inspect every number the tool uses

A damage estimate is only as credible as its inputs. The Data Check app (01_shared/07_datacheck.js) is a read-only inspector that lays out every unit cost, curve and threshold — with its source — so reviewers can audit the tool without reading code.

What it shows

SectionContent
Unit CostsThe 100 %-damage reconstruction cost of every class, each tagged with its provenance: [G] statistics-based, [E] recovery-budget request, [Furukawa] expert proposal — traceable to dataset_inventory.xlsx and the government source PDFs (DKI SHST 2025, HSPK, PermenPUPR, BPS).
DDF CurvesAll 13 depth–damage functions (JRC Huizinga 2017, Asia set) plotted from their control points.
Province RatioThe BPS IKK 2025 construction-cost index per province (DKI Jakarta = 1.00) applied to all region-priced classes.
ExposureThe 37 exposure assets (facilities, roads, land use, buildings) with feature counts, so missing or stale layers are caught before an event, not during one.
Sanity TestA worked example — one house in Jakarta flooded to 1.5 m — computed live, so the whole chain (cost × DDF × ratio) can be verified by hand.
Engine TestRegression checks of the valuation engine: pixel-wise DDF vs. scalar DDF agreement, a hand-calculated damage case, the WorldPop scale test (guards against a ×100 population bug), and old-vs-new engine totals on a reference event.

The app performs no batch computation and writes nothing — it reads constants and assets, so it opens instantly and is safe to share.
