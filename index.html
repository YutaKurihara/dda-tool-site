Disaster Damage Assessment (DDA Tool)

Satellite-based disaster mapping and damage estimation for Indonesia

The DDA Tool uses satellite data to identify disaster-affected areas and estimate direct physical damage to buildings, infrastructure, agriculture and other assets. Developed to support BNPB's disaster assessment activities, it combines satellite observations with Indonesian geospatial data to produce maps, damage estimates and reporting tables. The results help identify affected areas, prioritise field verification and support disaster damage reporting.

1 · Map Identify disaster-affected areas from satellite data — flood extent and depth, landslide scars, potential building collapse, and burned area.
2 · Estimate Overlay the affected area on 38 exposure layers and estimate affected assets, people and direct physical damage in rupiah.
3 · Report Export preliminary assessment tables formatted to support DIBI and Jitupasna reporting, and GIS data for further analysis.

The dashboard below shows stored assessments. It is a viewer: new events are analysed from the Earth Engine Code Editor (see the Manual). Active fire monitoring has its own screen on the Forest Fire page.

How to read the figures

Every number on the dashboard is either a satellite observation or a model estimate, and the two must not be confused — least of all estimated damage with reported damage. Reported damage comes from field assessment and BNPB's records; nothing on this dashboard replaces it.

FigureWhat it is
Disaster extentSatellite-detected area, plus — for floods — area inferred from terrain and the estimated water level (observed, inferred, unknown). For earthquakes, areas where satellite change suggests potential building collapse.
Flood depthModel-estimated depth from elevation data, not a measured water level.
Affected peoplePopulation located within the estimated affected area (BNPB InaRISK population grid).
Damage valueModel-estimated direct physical damage: exposed assets × replacement unit cost × depth–damage ratio. Indirect economic losses (lost income, business interruption) are not included.
Houses affectedEstimated count of building footprints touching the affected area; for landslides, earthquakes and fires, assets inside the detected area are assumed completely destroyed.
Assessment statusPreliminary assessment — requires field verification before official use.
What the tool does and does not do. Floods, landslides and earthquakes share one workflow: detect the affected area from satellite images, estimate damage from exposure and cost data, review and export. Fire has two separate functions — active fire monitoring (VIIRS thermal detections, no damage estimate) and burned area mapping (Sentinel-1 radar), the latter feeding the same damage estimation since 2026-10-17. The tool produces preliminary assessment tables formatted to support DIBI and Jitupasna reporting; additional information and field verification may be required for official submission.
