Earthquake building collapse — RV-PWTT

Building collapse changes how a pixel scatters radar and reflects light. The detector (02_event/03_earthquake_detection.js) measures that change with a pixel-wise t-statistic scaled by the pre-event variability only — a variant of the PWTT approach popularised by Ballinger's damage assessments in Ukraine and Turkey.

The statistic

For each pixel and each channel, a one-year pre-event stack gives a mean \( m_{\text{pre}} \) and standard deviation \( s_{\text{pre}} \); the post-event images give \( m_{\text{post}} \). The change statistic is

$$T \;=\; \frac{\lvert\, m_{\text{pre}} - m_{\text{post}} \,\rvert} {\,s_{\text{pre}} \cdot \sqrt{ \tfrac{1}{n_{\text{pre}}} + \tfrac{1}{n_{\text{post}}} }\,}$$

The denominator uses the pre-event spread alone — "reference variance", the RV in RV-PWTT. Because the post-event variance never enters, the statistic is meaningful from the very first post-event image. That is the property that matters in early response: no waiting for a stack of after-images, no training data, no damage labels.

  • SAR channels — Sentinel-1 VV and VH per orbit, Lee-filtered; \(T_{\text{sar}}\) is the maximum over channels.
  • Optical channels (optional) — six Sentinel-2 spectral/texture features, cloud-masked per pixel (Cloud Score+ cs_cdf ≥ 0.6, scenes < 40 % cloud); \(T_{\text{opt}}\) is the maximum over features.

Decision rule and operating points

A building pixel is flagged collapsed when either statistic exceeds its threshold (OR rule). A pixel whose optical side is lost to cloud falls back to the SAR test alone, so the map never has cloud holes. The verdict is deliberately binary — collapsed / not collapsed, no intermediate grade.

Both points were calibrated by a full threshold sweep against the 2023 Kahramanmaraş earthquake (Copernicus EMSR648, 4,619 graded buildings).

Calibration caveat: the thresholds are tuned on Turkish building stock and have not yet been re-calibrated for Indonesian construction. Treat absolute counts with care until Indonesian ground truth is incorporated.

Sensor modes

  • SAR + Optical — the calibrated configuration (OR rule).
  • SAR only — lighter and available from the first Sentinel-1 pass; uses the same \(T_{\text{sar}}\) threshold, slightly conservative on its own.

Display and outputs

The preview shows a smoothed \(T_{\text{sar}}\) heat map (yellow → red → purple, like the public PWTT app) wherever \(T_{\text{sar}} \geq 2.7\), the 99 % significance point — an intuitive "estimated damage" picture. The binary collapse extent that Stage 2 actually values is a separate toggleable layer.

As with landslides, the export is a mask only (quakemask_<id>, event ids prefixed quake_). The valuation engine applies the total-loss convention: collapsed footprint area × unit cost × 100 %. Building overlap happens in the damage engine, not in the detector.
